### › About me

I come from systems and networking (SMR, now ASIR) and I'm moving toward **offensive security and red teaming**. I'm interested in how things work under the hood: how an infrastructure holds together, where it breaks and how to attack it (and protect it).

I learn by building and breaking. My projects and writeups show it, each one walks through a full case, from initial enumeration to the objective, mapped to **MITRE ATT&CK** and with a detection perspective.

I complement the offensive side with **adversary analysis and threat intelligence**: OSINT actor profiling, open sources and passive querying only.

I'm currently studying my **ASIR** degree while solving labs in controlled environments (Hack The Box, TryHackMe, CTFs) and taking part in **Bug Bounty / VDP** programs.
